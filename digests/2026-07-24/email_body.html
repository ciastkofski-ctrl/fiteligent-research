TL;DR — Top 5 Highlights This Week

  1. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ICS therapy loses superiority in obese/overweight asthmatic children — A systematic review of 9 studies (1 RCT, 5 cohorts, 3 case-control) finds that inhaled corticosteroid monotherapy or step-up regimens, the standard pharmacological backbone of pediatric asthma management, appear to lose their comparative advantage over other controllers in children with overweight or obesity. No meta-analysis was possible due to methodological heterogeneity.

  2. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Vitamin D (4,800 IU/day × 8 weeks) reduced anxiety but not overall depression scores in youth aged 10–24 — In a partially randomized trial (n = 108), randomized vitamin D supplementation did not significantly improve Hamilton Depression Rating Scale scores vs placebo (B = −0.30, p = 0.698), but did reduce anxiety on the GAD-7 (B = −1.78, 95% CI −3.35 to −0.32, p = 0.026). A subgroup with moderate-to-severe depression showed benefit.

  3. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Poor sleep quality affects 67% of tension-type headache patients — A meta-analysis of 27 observational studies finds a pooled prevalence of poor sleep quality (PSQI) of 67% (95% CI 53–79%) in adults with tension-type headache, with strong associations with depressive symptoms and headache burden.

  4. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Plant-forward diet in toddlers accelerates bone turnover without altering nutrient intake — A 10-month cluster-RCT in Finnish ECEC centres (n = 116 children) finds elevated bone formation (S-BAP, p = 0.04) and resorption markers (S-TRAP5b, p = 0.02) in the plant-forward group vs controls, with no detectable change in calcium, vitamin D, phosphorus, or protein intake. Clinical significance for long-term bone health remains unclear.

  5. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Dutasteride vs finasteride: comparable hair density gains, stronger PSA suppression with dutasteride — A 24-week single-blind RCT in 46 Iranian men with moderate-to-severe AGA finds no significant difference in trichoscopic hair density or thickness between finasteride 1 mg/day and dutasteride 0.5 mg/day, but dutasteride produced a greater serum PSA reduction (from 0.63 ± 0.18 to 0.42 ± 0.22).
