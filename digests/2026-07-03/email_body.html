  3. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Heavy lifting protects peri-menopausal bone and muscle — A 9-month RCT (80–85% 1RM + drop landings, twice weekly) increased total body lean mass by 0.90 kg (95% CI 0.08–1.72), leg power by 97 W (95% CI 2–193), and lumbar spine aBMD by 0.020 g/cm² versus waitlisted controls.
  4. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Multivitamin and COVID-19 — In a large double-blind RCT (n = 21,442), daily MVM did not significantly reduce COVID-19 incidence (HR 0.93, 95% CI 0.81–1.07), but per-protocol analysis showed reduced odds of symptomatic COVID-19 (OR 0.60, 95% CI 0.37–0.99) in adherent participants.
  5. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ PCOS and cognition: small, non-significant effects — A 22-study meta-analysis (136,008 women with PCOS, 333,895 controls) finds pooled correlations for executive function (r = −0.12), attention (r = −0.13), and working memory (r = −0.14) all cross zero — no reliable cognitive impairment signal.
