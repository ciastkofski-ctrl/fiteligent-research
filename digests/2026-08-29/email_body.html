TL;DR — Top 5 Highlights

  1. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Short sleep → obesity in youth (meta-analysis, OR 1.25): Across 10 pooled studies, short sleep duration was associated with 25% higher odds of obesity in children and adolescents aged 6–18; the effect was slightly stronger in teens (OR 1.38) than younger children (OR 1.20).

  2. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Vitamin D doesn't shrink waistlines in shift workers (RCT, n=198): Four months of cholecalciferol at 14,000 or 28,000 IU/week successfully raised serum 25(OH)D but produced zero significant change in waist circumference or waist-to-height ratio vs placebo.

  3. ⭐⭐⭐ Adipose-derived mitochondrial vesicles activate cGAS-STING in joints: A mechanistic study identifies a novel EV-Mito-mtDNA → cGAS-STING axis linking body fat to cartilage degeneration; HIIT selectively reduced adiposity and was associated with lower synovial mtDNA and improved OA symptoms.

  4. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ PEA supplementation preserves bioactive lipid tone post-exercise: In a 10-person double-blind crossover RCT, PEA (700 mg/d) attenuated the post-eccentric-exercise decline in 12 distinct lipid mediators within skeletal muscle, with MCTR3 consistently higher in the PEA condition.

  5. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Nutritional interventions modulate neuroinflammation in AD/MCI (systematic review, 22 studies): Omega-3 fatty acids showed the most consistent clinical evidence for reducing TNF-α, IL-1β, IL-6, and NF-κB/NLRP3 signaling; ketogenic diet evidence was predominantly preclinical; B-vitamins, vitamin D, and microbiota strategies had limited eligible studies.
