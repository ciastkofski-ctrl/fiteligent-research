## Obesity & Metabolic Health

### [Association between unhealthy snack food and beverage consumption and the nutritional status of Nepalese children and adolescents: A systematic review](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0352341)

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ · Parajuli et al. · PLoS One (2026) · systematic review

- Systematic review of literature from PubMed, Google Scholar, Web of Science, and Scopus assessing associations between unhealthy snack/beverage consumption and nutritional status in Nepalese children and adolescents across urban, peri-urban, and rural settings.
- Urban areas: high frequency of unhealthy snack/beverage consumption; significantly associated with higher odds of overweight or obesity.
- Sub-urban areas: moderate consumption levels, exceeding rural but generally below urban; rural consumption remains lowest but is increasing due to marketing expansion and improved access.
- Dual nutritional burden documented: majority of Nepalese children maintain normal weight, yet underweight and stunting remain significant public health issues in rural areas, while overweight and obesity are growing concerns in urban settings.
- Authors note the pattern reflects ongoing disparities in food diversity, accessibility, and dietary quality between rural and urban Nepal; findings are consistent with broader nutrition-transition literature in low- and middle-income countries.

### [From dual to quintuple agonism for next-generation pharmacology to treat obesity and type 2 diabetes: synergistic incretin and nuclear receptor signaling](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40842-026-00321-4)

⭐⭐⭐ · Santulli · Cardiovasc Diabetol Endocrinol Rep (2026) · narrative/mechanistic review

- Describes GLP-1–GIP–lanifibranor: a unimolecular conjugate combining GLP-1R/GIPR co-agonism with pan-PPAR (α/δ/γ) activation via receptor-guided intracellular delivery to incretin receptor-expressing cells, designed to limit systemic off-target PPAR exposure.
- In obese mouse models, the conjugate produced greater reductions in body weight, adiposity, food intake, and hyperglycemia compared with semaglutide, GLP-1–GIP co-agonism alone, or lanifibranor alone.
- Mechanistic analyses identified PPARδ signaling as a principal mediator of glycemic improvement independent of weight loss; delivery was confirmed as receptor-dependent.
- Unlike unconjugated lanifibranor, the conjugate did not induce anemia, fluid retention, renal dysfunction, or adipocyte differentiation in these preclinical models.
- Authors explicitly flag "substantial translational uncertainties regarding clinical efficacy, safety, and long-term applicability"; no human data presented.

## Strength & Hypertrophy

### [Unlocking Greater Load Potential: How Cluster Sets Enable Higher External Loads](https://doi.org/10.1519/JSC.0000000000005579)

⭐⭐⭐ · Nagatani et al. · J Strength Cond Res (2026) · randomized repeated-measures crossover

- 20 strength-trained individuals (relative 1RM: 1.21 ± 0.16 kg·kg⁻¹) performed 3 sets of 9 power cleans under three randomized protocols: TRAD (70% 1RM, no intraset rest), CLU-3 (85% 1RM, 30-s rest every 3 reps), and CLU-1 (90% 1RM, 30-s inter-repetition rest).
- Primary outcome: barbell peak velocity (PV). TRAD produced the highest absolute PV overall, but velocity loss across the set was −0.17 ± 0.02 m/s; CLU-3 velocity loss was −0.11 ± 0.03 m/s; CLU-1 velocity loss was −0.03 ± 0.03 m/s.
- Waveform analysis (statistical parametric mapping) of vertical and horizontal barbell displacement-time curves showed significant changes across the set in TRAD (p ≤ 0.05), indicating technique degradation; CLU-3 and CLU-1 maintained relatively consistent barbell trajectories.
- CLU-1 and CLU-3 showed lower absolute PV than TRAD due to heavier loads, but preserved lifting mechanics and attenuated fatigue accumulation across the high-volume bout.
- Analysis used linear mixed-effects models; no long-term adaptation data reported; acute kinematic study only.

### [Do probiotics modulate dietary intake? Pilot data from a randomized controlled sub-study of the ProBioHRV clinical trial in patients with depression and healthy controls](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0350801)

⭐⭐⭐⭐ · Putz et al. · PLoS One (2026) · double-blind placebo-controlled RCT sub-study · n = 53

- Double-blind, placebo-controlled RCT sub-study; 53 participants (23 with major depression [MD], 30 healthy controls [HC]) received a multi-strain probiotic or placebo twice daily for 3 months; dietary intake assessed at baseline and 3 follow-up time points using the Vienna Food Record (VFR).
- Analysis: mixed ANOVAs for repeated measures; data analyzed using nut.s® software.
- Significant three-way interactions (time × intervention × diagnosis) found for: vitamin D intake, dietary variety, folic acid, and diversity.
- In HC, probiotic supplementation associated with higher vitamin D intake after 1 week; in MD, a similar increase observed at 3 months (trend level, p = 0.058). Probiotic group showed lower dietary variety and diversity scores at several time points.
- Folic acid intake was lower in MD vs. HC across all time points regardless of intervention. Authors describe all findings as descriptive and hypothesis-generating; larger powered studies with objective nutritional and microbiome measures recommended.

### [Clinical efficacy of acupuncture combined with estazolam on chronic insomnia](https://doi.org/10.13702/j.1000-0607.20250215)

⭐⭐⭐⭐ · Shen et al. · Zhen Ci Yan Jiu (2026) · RCT · n = 64

- Single-blind RCT; 64 patients with chronic insomnia randomized 1:1 into control group (estazolam alone, orally at bedtime, 4 weeks) and treatment group (estazolam + acupuncture every 2 days, 3×/week, 4 weeks).
- Outcomes assessed: Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index (PSQI), Self-Anxiety Scale (SAS), objective sleep indicators (total sleep duration, sleep latency, sleep efficiency), Wechsler Memory Scale (WMS-RC), and TCM symptom scores.
- Total effective rate: 87.10% in the acupuncture + estazolam group vs. 67.74% in the estazolam-only group (p-value not specified in abstract).
- Combined treatment associated with improvements in overall sleep quality, anxiety status, memory level, and TCM symptom scores compared with estazolam alone.
- Single-center Chinese study; use of traditional TCM diagnostic criteria and outcome tools (WMS-RC, TCM symptom scores) limits direct comparability with Western insomnia research. No washout or long-term follow-up described.

## Skin & Hair Health

### [Comparing fractional CO₂ laser and needling-based modalities in facial acne scar treatment: a comprehensive systematic review and meta-analysis](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10103-026-04905-5)

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ · Batool et al. · Lasers Med Sci (2026) · systematic review and meta-analysis · 17 studies

- PRISMA-compliant systematic review and meta-analysis; databases searched: PubMed, Cochrane, Google Scholar, ClinicalTrials.gov; 17 studies included comparing fractional CO₂ laser vs. needling-based monotherapy for facial atrophic acne scars in adults; random-effects model used (RevMan).
- Efficacy — mean scar score reduction: SMD −1.08 (95% CI: −2.48 to 0.32, I² = 97%) — non-significant with very high heterogeneity. Mean quantitative scar reduction: SMD 0.12 (95% CI: −0.80 to 1.04, I² = 92%) — non-significant, high heterogeneity.
- Categorical treatment success: significantly higher for CO₂ laser (RR 1.10, 95% CI: 1.01–1.20, I² = 0%).
- Safety — post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH): CO₂ laser showed significantly higher risk vs. standard microneedling (RR 3.04, 95% CI: 1.49–6.21, I² = 3%). Pain scores: CO₂ laser significantly lower than RF microneedling (SMD −0.74, 95% CI: −1.27 to −0.21, I² = 0%). No significant differences between modalities for erythema, crusting/scabbing, or edema.
- Clinical implications: authors recommend considering patient skin type, scar type, and complication risk in modality selection; CO₂ laser may offer marginal efficacy advantage but with meaningfully higher PIH risk — particularly relevant for darker Fitzpatrick skin types.
- High heterogeneity in primary efficacy outcomes (I² = 97%, 92%) substantially limits confidence in pooled estimates; results should be interpreted with caution.
