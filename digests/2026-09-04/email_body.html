TL;DR — Top 5 Highlights

  1. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Load-velocity profiles for 1RM prediction — A meta-analysis of 63 sensor-validation studies and 38 prediction-model studies found pooled ICC = 0.90–0.91 for velocity-based 1RM prediction, with LPTs outperforming IMUs; large heterogeneity in lower-body exercises limits practical conclusions.

  2. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Ketone supplementation in PCOS — Meta-analysis shows significant reductions in BMI (IV −13.08), fasting glucose (IV −10.83), insulin (IV −11.87), LH, and androgens; high heterogeneity (I² = 93%) in glucose and insulin outcomes warrants caution in interpretation.

  3. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Daytime light supplementation in older adults — In a 202-participant RCT across three EU cities, morning light use (onset before 10:00) was associated with greater daytime activity and lower rest-activity fragmentation; broader naturalistic light exposure correlated with reduced metabolic disorder incidence.

  4. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Vitamin D and lumbar spinal fusion — Meta-analysis of 9 studies found adequate vitamin D status or supplementation associated with 25% higher fusion success (RR 1.25, 95% CI 1.13–1.38) and 55% lower fusion failure risk (RR 0.45, 95% CI 0.27–0.76); patient-reported outcomes showed no significant benefit.

  5. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Sublingual minoxidil for androgenetic alopecia in transgender patients on testosterone — 24-week double-blind RCT (n = 46) showed +15.7 hairs/cm² improvement at midfrontal site; well-tolerated without interfering with masculinization.
