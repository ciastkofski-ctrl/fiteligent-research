## Obesity & Metabolic Health

### [Cardiac Autonomic Dysfunction in Children and Adolescents with Obesity: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis](https://doi.org/10.1590/1984-0462/2026/44/2026017)
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ · Dornelles et al. · Rev Paul Pediatr (2026) · n = 1,590 · meta-analysis

- 18 observational studies (1,590 participants) comparing short-term resting HRV in children/adolescents with excess weight vs. normal-weight controls; protocol pre-registered in PROSPERO (CRD420251005501).
- Random-effects meta-analysis: RMSSD SMD −1.00 (95% CI −1.68 to −0.31) and pNN50 SMD −0.48 (95% CI −0.68 to −0.28) — both lower in excess-weight group, indicating reduced parasympathetic modulation.
- LF/HF ratio was higher in the excess-weight group (SMD +0.70; 95% CI 0.30 to 1.10), consistent with sympathetic predominance.
- No significant between-group differences observed for SDNN or mean NN interval.
- Between-group BMI difference significantly moderated LF/HF (β = −0.15; p = 0.045) but not RMSSD; leave-one-out analyses supported directional robustness; pNN50 showed the most consistent reduction across studies.

### [Caregiver App Use and Child Behavioral Adherence in Relation to Obesity-Related Outcomes in an mHealth App-Assisted Childhood Obesity Intervention: Secondary Analysis of a Cluster Randomized Trial](https://doi.org/10.2196/89548)
⭐⭐⭐⭐ · Yan et al. · JMIR Mhealth Uhealth (2026) · RCT (secondary analysis)

- Secondary analysis of 684 child-caregiver dyads in the intervention arm of the DECH-children cluster-RCT, implemented across 3 socioeconomically diverse regions in China; 9-month follow-up.
- Caregiver app-use adherence operationalized as app-use frequency and total usage duration (app-recorded); child behavioral adherence measured via weekly monitoring scores covering diet, screen time, and physical activity.
- Higher caregiver app-use frequency and duration were both significantly associated with reductions in children's BMI (β = −0.19 per adherence unit reported); linear mixed models used.
- Joint adherence (high caregiver + high child) associated with more favorable changes in both weight status and physical fitness outcomes compared with low-adherence dyads.
- Causal directionality cannot be established from secondary/observational analysis; selection into high adherence may reflect unmeasured motivation.

### [Satiety Responsiveness Following Dietary Exposure to Ultra-Processed Foods Differing in Texture-Derived Eating Rate: A Secondary Analysis of a Randomized Controlled Crossover Diet Intervention](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00394-026-04111-7)
⭐⭐⭐⭐ · van Bruinessen et al. · Eur J Nutr (2026) · n = 32 · RCT (crossover, secondary analysis)

- Randomized crossover design; n = 32 (mean age 27 ± 2 y; BMI 23.6 ± 2.3 kg/m²); two 14-day high-UPF diet periods compared with baseline; pre-registered at ClinicalTrials.gov (NCT06113146).
- Small but significant aggregate difference observed in subjective satiety AUC: higher hunger and lower fullness AUCs following high-UPF diets vs. baseline; no significant differences at individual timepoints (all p > 0.05).
- Satiety hormone responses to a fixed-calorie load did not differ between baseline and after 14-day high-UPF exposure (all p > 0.05).
- Fasting-state appetite ratings remained stable across days within each diet period on both UPF conditions.
- Results do not support the hypothesis that short-term (14-day) high-UPF exposure meaningfully disrupts satiety responsiveness; small sample, lean participants, and secondary-analysis status limit generalizability.

## Longevity

### [Inotodiol Prevents Age-Related Muscle Wasting by Restoring Mitochondrial Function Through Liver X Receptor Beta Signaling](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41392-026-02935-7)
⭐⭐⭐ · You et al. · Signal Transduct Target Ther (2026) · preclinical

- Investigated the mycosterol inotodiol (Ino) — derived from *Inonotus obliquus* (chaga mushroom) — as a candidate for mitigating sarcopenia; experimental models included aged mice and dexamethasone-induced atrophy in vitro.
- Ino treatment improved muscle mass and function in aged mice and attenuated DEX-induced atrophy; increased mitochondrial content, elevated oxidative capacity, reduced lipid accumulation, and decreased oxidative stress were reported.
- Mechanistically, Ino was found to activate liver X receptor β (LXRβ) and promote its interaction with PPARδ, thereby increasing SIRT3 transcription and activating the LXRβ/SIRT3/PGC-1α axis.
- Ino also attenuated palmitic acid-induced lipotoxicity in muscle cells by restoring lipid metabolism.
- Entirely preclinical (mouse/cell); no human data; no effect sizes or statistics reported in abstract.

⭐⭐⭐ · Calabrò et al. · Expert Opin Ther Targets (2026) · review

- No original data; no meta-analytic pooling; conclusions are narrative synthesis of existing literature.
- NAD⁺ precursor findings discussed but no specific effect sizes provided in the abstract.

## Strength & Hypertrophy

### [Resistance Training Combined with Creatine Supplementation: A Three-Level Meta-Analysis of Multidimensional Outcomes from Strength Enhancement to Body Composition Remodeling](https://doi.org/10.1080/15502783.2026.2718316)
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ · Wang et al. · J Int Soc Sports Nutr (2026) · meta-analysis

- Systematic search of PubMed, Web of Science, Cochrane, Embase, EBSCO through April 21, 2025, plus backward citation tracking; 63 RCTs included; three-level random-effects model in metafor (R); Hedge's g used as summary effect size.
- Creatine + RT significantly enhanced muscle strength (upper- and lower-body) and lean body mass compared with RT alone; fat mass was not significantly affected.
- Modest increase in total body mass was primarily attributable to lean mass gains, with a slight accompanying reduction in body fat percentage.
- Age appeared associated with magnitude of strength effects in subgroup/meta-regression analyses, though this is flagged as exploratory given uneven representation of age subgroups.
- Intervention-protocol variables (duration, weekly frequency, dosing strategy, daily dose, supplementation timing) did not significantly moderate outcomes; nutritional intake and training background were heterogeneously and incompletely reported across trials.

### [Correlating Vocal Fold Bowing and Thyroarytenoid Myocyte Dimensions in an Aging Rat Model](https://doi.org/10.1002/lary.70916)
⭐⭐⭐ · Miller et al. · Laryngoscope (2026) · n = 24 (rats) · animal study

- 24 Sprague-Dawley rats across three age groups (4-, 18-, and 24-month) used to examine laryngeal aging; endoscopic video collected and Bowing Index (BI) independently scored by two reviewers; thyroarytenoid muscle histomorphometry performed.
- Bowing Index was greater in 18- and 24-month rats vs. 4-month (MD 7.15, 95% CI 4.39–9.90; and MD 4.26, 95% CI 1.50–7.01, respectively), paralleling clinical age-related vocal atrophy (ARVA) findings in humans.
- Mean minimum Feret diameter (MFD) increased with age: 19.8 ± 1.3 µm at 4 months → 22.1 ± 1.7 µm at 18 months → 22.5 ± 1.5 µm at 24 months (4 vs. 18, p = 0.014; 4 vs. 24, p = 0.005; 18 vs. 24, p = 0.87).
- Aged rats showed increased myofiber size (cross-sectional area also increased), contrary to the atrophy hypothesis; authors suggest compensatory hypertrophy or fiber-type shifts as possible mechanisms.
- Animal model; direct translation to human laryngeal pathology requires further investigation; n per group not explicitly stated.

### [Low Prevalence of Exploratory Biochemical Patterns Compatible with Osteomalacia in a Large Cohort of Participants with Low Serum 25-Hydroxyvitamin D Levels](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00223-026-01612-9)
⭐⭐⭐⭐ · Jorde et al. · Calcif Tissue Int (2026) · RCT + cross-sectional

- Cross-sectional component: 423 participants from the population-based Tromsø Study with low vitamin D status (mean serum 25-OHD = 34 nmol/L); RCT component: 382 completers (194 vitamin D, 188 placebo); trial registered NCT02750293.
- Vitamin D RCT arm received 100,000 IU loading dose followed by 20,000 IU/week for 4 months; outcomes: parathyroid hormone (PTH), calcium, alkaline phosphatase (ALP), and bone-specific ALP.
- Zero participants met the strictest biochemical definition of osteomalacia (simultaneous elevation of PTH and ALP + low calcium); with the most permissive exploratory biochemical pattern, prevalence was 8.3%.
- In the RCT, vitamin D supplementation significantly reduced plasma PTH and ALP and increased serum calcium compared with placebo.
- Authors note findings cannot determine the true prevalence of osteomalacia or establish an appropriate 25-OHD threshold without validation against bone histomorphometry (biopsy).

## Sleep & Recovery

### [Pharmacological Treatment of Sleep Disorder Phenotypes in Parkinson's Disease: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Randomised Controlled Trials](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00415-026-14158-5)
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ · Canever et al. · J Neurol (2026) · n = 1,913 · meta-analysis

- 29 RCTs (1,913 participants; 2002–2025) identified across six databases without restrictions; random-effects pooling; risk of bias assessed with Cochrane RoB 2 tool; PROSPERO registration CRD420261412614.
- Only 17.2% of included trials were at low overall risk of bias.
- For excessive daytime sleepiness (EDS; k = 6): pooled Epworth Sleepiness Scale (ESS) estimate was directionally favourable but non-significant (MD 1.75; 95% CI −0.26 to 3.75); very high statistical heterogeneity noted.
- Chronobiotic agents and dopaminergic receptor agonists showed directional improvement in overall sleep quality (low-certainty evidence); evidence for EDS, insomnia symptoms, and REM sleep behaviour disorder (RBD) insufficient for definitive recommendations.
- Authors explicitly caution against over-interpreting pooled estimates given very high statistical heterogeneity and call for phenotype-specific RCTs with harmonised outcome measures.

### [Improving Adaptation in Male High-Altitude Workers Through Pressurization: A Randomized Controlled Trial on Sleep Architecture](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00420-026-02229-z)
⭐⭐⭐⭐ · Shan et al. · Int Arch Occup Environ Health (2026) · n = 24 · RCT

- 5-week RCT; 24 male workers residing at 3,500 m altitude randomized to Pressurization group (n = 12) or Control group (n = 12); pressurization group slept in an environmental simulator at sequentially lower simulated altitudes (3,000 m → 2,500 m → 2,000 m) over 3 weeks while working at 3,500 m during the day.
- Significant Group × Time interaction for nocturnal oxygenation: Mean SpO₂ (F(4,88) = 28.36, p < 0.001); Minimum SpO₂ (F(4,88) = 7.16, p < 0.001); dose-dependent increases in both metrics peaking at simulated 2,000 m.
- Deep sleep percentage increased significantly in pressurization group at 2,000 m (Group × Time effect: +3.25%, p = 0.010).
- Arousal index (ArI) reduced by −5.42 events/h at 2,500 m and −7.01 events/h at 2,000 m in the pressurization group (both p < 0.001).
- Epworth Sleepiness Scale scores decreased significantly in pressurization group during descent (p < 0.01); no significant changes in total sleep time, REM sleep, or wake percentage; all-male sample limits generalizability; small n.

## Skin & Hair Health

### [Retinoic Acid in Health and Disease](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41392-026-03055-y)
⭐⭐⭐ · De Angelis et al. · Signal Transduct Target Ther (2026) · review

- Comprehensive narrative review of retinoic acid (RA) — the active metabolite of vitamin A — covering developmental, homeostatic, and pathological roles across organ systems.
- Skin-relevant content: RA contributes to epithelial integrity, stem-cell regulation, immune homeostasis, and metabolic balance in adult tissues; disruption of RA synthesis, degradation, or receptor signaling is implicated in a broad spectrum of pathologies.
- In oncology: retinoid-based interventions have transformed treatment of acute promyelocytic leukemia; broader application constrained by resistance mechanisms, pharmacokinetic limitations, tissue-specific effects, and dual (differentiation-inducing vs. stemness-maintaining) signaling character.
- Authors propose concept of "retinoid endotypes" — tissue- and stage-specific RA signaling states shaped by metabolic niches, chromatin state, and microenvironmental organization — as a framework for patient stratification and precision retinoid therapy.
- No original data; no quantitative outcomes; conclusions represent narrative synthesis.

### [Epigenetic Clocks Can Differentiate Between Exogenous and Endogenous Skin Ageing](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13148-026-02230-w)
⭐⭐⭐ · Shore et al. · Clin Epigenetics (2026) · n > 400 skin samples · observational

- Exogenous and endogenous skin epigenetic clocks trained in >400 skin samples; tested alongside the Horvath "Skin and Blood" clock for association with chronological age and (age-acceleration-adjusted) sun-exposure parameters and gene expression.
- Exogenous Skin clock ages were significantly older in sun-exposed arm samples vs. donor-matched sun-protected samples in both epidermal (n = 24) and cultured fibroblast (n = 8) samples (p < 0.001).
- Exogenous clock age acceleration significantly correlated with sun-seeking behaviour (ρ = 0.18, p = 0.004) and with mRNA levels in whole-skin abdomen samples (n = 364; 202 genes with q < 0.05); endogenous and Horvath clocks showed neither association.
- In 44 facial skin swabs, exogenous age acceleration correlated significantly with facial photodamage grading (ρ = 0.33, p = 0.028).
- Endogenous and Horvath clocks showed stronger correlation with chronological age overall; this is an observational study — no intervention data; directionality of gene expression associations cannot be inferred.

### [A Retinoic Acid-Rfx6 Cascade Modulates Wnt/Bmp-Dependent Gut Tube Patterning: Insight into the Molecular Basis of Mitchell-Riley Syndrome](https://doi.org/10.1242/dev.205552)
⭐⭐⭐ · Rankin et al. · Development (2026) · developmental/molecular biology

- Used human pluripotent stem cell (hPSC) cultures and *Xenopus* embryo models to demonstrate that retinoic acid (RA) from lateral plate mesoderm directly activates transcription factor Rfx6 in posterior foregut endoderm.
- Rfx6 promotes posterior foregut identity while suppressing Wnt-dependent hindgut and Bmp-dependent pharyngeal fates through multiple direct and indirect mechanisms.
- Rfx6 directly activates foregut TFs (onecut1, pdx1) and Wnt antagonists (sfrp2/5); directly suppresses hindgut TF cdx2 and pharyngeal TFs nkx2-5 and nkx2-6; indirectly restricts Wnt and Bmp ligand expression.
- Study provides mechanistic insight into Mitchell-Riley Syndrome congenital anomalies, caused by RFX6 mutations.
- Exclusively developmental/molecular biology; no direct adult skin or hair relevance; placed in skin/hair theme based on input classification but thematic fit is limited — see Run Notes.
