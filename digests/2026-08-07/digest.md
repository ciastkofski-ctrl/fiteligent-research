## TL;DR — Top 5 Highlights This Week

1. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Short sleep raises hypertension risk in women** — A 12-cohort meta-analysis (n not reported per study) found short sleep duration associated with hypertension risk (RR 1.10, 95% CI 1.06–1.13); the optimal sleep duration was ~7.5 hours/day (L-shaped dose-response curve).
2. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Ketone monoester partially protects cognitive performance at altitude** — In a double-blind crossover RCT (n = 16 males), R-BD R-βHB KME (650 mg/kg) attenuated hypoxia-induced mean reaction time decline by 25.5 ms (p = 0.002) vs placebo at simulated 6,096 m altitude.
3. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Intradialytic creatine supplementation is feasible and safe in hemodialysis patients** — A dose-escalation pilot RCT (n = 16) confirmed significant increases in intra-erythrocytic creatine at 1.5 mmol/L and 2.0 mmol/L (overall treatment effect p < 0.001) with one dropout across all groups.
4. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Herbst appliance reduces AHI in mandibular retrognathia with OSA** — An 8-month RCT (n = 46) found AHI significantly improved in the Herbst group (p = 0.031) and supine AHI (p = 0.001); Twin Block did not reach statistical significance for AHI.
5. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Sleep monitoring + telephone guidance improves insomnia scores in older adults** — A three-arm RCT (n = 229, mean age 78.5 years) demonstrated significant group-by-time interaction for Athens Insomnia Scale scores (p = 0.002), with improvements at 3 months vs no change in the device-only control group.

### [Projected budget impact of pharmacotherapy scenarios for obesity and related conditions in Peru: A national survey analysis of 76,819 participants](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.obpill.2026.100308)
⭐⭐⭐ · Vera Ponce VJ et al. · Obes Pillars (2026) · n = 76,819 records · observational cross-sectional + budget impact analysis

- Pooled ENDES (2022–2024) survey data covering 76,819 records; three population strata identified: 7.54 million adults with BMI-defined obesity, 9.04 million meeting a "broad criterion," and 3.67 million meeting "pragmatic priority."
- At 100% coverage under the broad criterion, projected annual medication-acquisition budgets were S/142.95 billion for liraglutide 3.0 mg, S/23.65 billion for naltrexone/bupropion ER (IETSI-reported price), and S/26.83 billion for phentermine/topiramate ER.
- Pragmatic-priority budgets were S/57.95 billion (liraglutide), S/9.59 billion (naltrexone/bupropion), and S/10.88 billion (phentermine/topiramate).
- Semaglutide 2.4 mg was modeled as a supplementary non-governmental retail scenario: S/16,804.86 per patient in year 1; S/18,135.52 per patient in a maintenance year.
- Deterministic scenario design; does not account for adherence, clinical outcomes, or cost-effectiveness; projected budgets reflect gross medication-acquisition costs only.

### [Hypothalamic syndrome and acquired hypothalamic obesity: from fragmented care to integrated multidisciplinary management](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11102-026-01743-0)
⭐⭐⭐ · Biagetti B et al. · Pituitary (2026) · narrative review

- Narrative review covering acquired hypothalamic obesity (AHO) as the weight-related manifestation of the broader hypothalamic syndrome (HS); scope includes pathophysiology, diagnostic frameworks, prevention, and emerging therapies.
- AHO is characterized by rapid weight gain and multisystem dysfunction following hypothalamic damage; associated disturbances include pituitary dysfunction, impaired energy expenditure, sleep disruption, behavioral changes, and thermoregulation defects.
- Authors propose a three-layer diagnostic model integrating: (1) etiological factors, (2) longitudinal anthropometric and metabolic assessment, and (3) domain-specific clinical evaluation; BMI-alone assessment is identified as insufficient.
- Preventive strategies remain underexplored; hypothalamus-sparing surgical interventions, early weight trajectory monitoring, and management within specialized Pituitary Tumor Centers of Excellence are highlighted as critical.
- Emerging therapies targeting the melanocortin pathway (MC4R agonists) are identified as showing "promising results"; conventional treatments described as having only modest evidence; no quantitative effect sizes reported for any specific intervention.

## Longevity

### [Effect of Acute Ingestion of a Ketone Monoester on Psychomotor Vigilance During Acute Severe Hypoxia Exposure](https://doi.org/10.1093/milmed/usag123)
⭐⭐⭐⭐ · McClure TS et al. · Mil Med (2026) · n = 16 males · double-blind crossover RCT

- Double-blind crossover RCT; n = 16 male participants; two sessions each with psychomotor vigilance test (PVT) in normoxia (20.9% O₂, 0 m, 15 min) followed by acute hypoxia (9.7% O₂, simulated 6,096 m altitude, 15 min).
- Intervention: (R)-3-hydroxybutyl (R)-3-hydroxybutyrate (R-BD R-βHB) ketone monoester (KME) 650 mg/kg body mass vs taste-matched placebo; ingested ~30 minutes before hypoxia exposure.
- Blood R-βHB concentration rose rapidly and was sustained >3 mM throughout KME condition (p < 0.001); blood glucose was ~20 mg/dL lower in KME vs placebo throughout (p < 0.01).
- Primary cognitive finding: hypoxia-induced decline in mean reaction time was attenuated with KME, producing a 25.5 ms faster mean reaction time vs placebo in hypoxia (95% CI: 10.7–40.3 ms; p = 0.002).
- No significant between-condition differences for other PVT outcome measures (e.g., lapses, fastest 10% RT); heart rate response to hypoxia did not differ between KME and placebo (p = 0.87); small all-male sample limits generalizability.

### [SIRT1 in brain aging: molecular mechanisms and therapeutic potential of pharmacological and natural modulators](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10522-026-10480-7)
⭐⭐⭐ · Mosalam EM et al. · Biogerontology (2026) · narrative review

- Narrative review covering SIRT1's role in brain aging; SIRT1 is a class III histone deacetylase (NAD⁺-dependent) with high expression in brain regions involved in energy expenditure and metabolic activity.
- SIRT1 proposed mechanistic roles: genomic stability maintenance, reduction of oxidative stress-induced DNA injury, modulation of neuronal senescence, and neuroprotection via downstream anti-inflammatory and antioxidant pathways.
- Identified SIRT1 activators with neuroprotective evidence: resveratrol, metformin, and statins; mechanism described as modulation of inflammatory and oxidative stress signaling pathways.
- Review is narrative, not systematic; no meta-analytic pooling of effect sizes; no formal quality assessment of included studies reported.
- Brain-specific SIRT1 expression in energy-regulating regions (e.g., hypothalamus, hippocampus) identified as relevant to both cognitive aging and metabolic brain function.

## Strength & Hypertrophy

### [Ultrasound-guided iliopsoas plane block, the effects on postoperative pain, opioid consumption, and quadriceps strength in adults undergoing hip surgery: A Systematic Review](https://doi.org/10.52628/92.2.14859)
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ · Kuijper N et al. · Acta Orthop Belg (2026) · systematic review (7 RCTs + 2 case series)

- Systematic review following PRISMA guidelines; 9 studies included (7 RCTs, 2 case series); database search to May 2025; population: adults undergoing hip surgery under general anesthesia.
- Intervention: ultrasound-guided iliopsoas plane block (IPB) compared to sham, conventional nerve blocks (e.g., femoral nerve block [FNB]), or no block.
- Pain outcomes: 1 of the included RCTs reported significantly lower pain scores with IPB vs sham; remaining studies found no significant differences vs sham or other blocks.
- Opioid consumption: 2 of 4 RCTs showed significantly reduced postoperative opioid consumption with IPB vs comparator (p < 0.001); 2 RCTs showed no difference.
- Quadriceps strength: 1 RCT showed superior quadriceps strength in IPB group vs FNB group at multiple postoperative time points (p < 0.05); other studies showed no significant difference or even strength reductions vs sham; overall evidence for motor-sparing benefit is mixed.

> **Note:** This study's primary relevance is perioperative anesthesia/analgesia, not sports strength & hypertrophy. Included under this section due to theme classification in source data; clinical applicability to general strength training is indirect.

## Supplements

### [Feasibility, safety and tolerability of intradialytic creatine supplementation in hemodialysis: A double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled pilot study](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0354883)
⭐⭐⭐⭐ · Doorenbos CSE et al. · PLoS One (2026) · n = 16 · double-blind RCT (pilot, dose-escalation)

- Double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled pilot study (ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT05148208); n = 16 hemodialysis patients (age 27–78 years, 9 female) divided into four sequential dose groups (0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0 mmol/L creatine in dialysate); block randomization 3:1 (creatine:placebo) per group; 6-week intervention.
- Rationale: hemodialysis patients are at risk of creatine deficiency due to impaired endogenous synthesis and dialysis-related losses; creatine deficiency is associated with lower health-related quality of life and higher mortality in this population.
- Primary outcomes: changes in pre-dialytic intra-erythrocytic creatine and plasma creatine concentrations; analyzed via linear mixed models.
- Intra-erythrocytic creatine changes vs placebo at 6 weeks: +399 µmol/L (95% CI: −269 to +1066) at 0.5 mmol/L; +685 µmol/L (95% CI: +17 to +1352) at 1.0 mmol/L; +1146 µmol/L (95% CI: +431 to +1766) at 1.5 mmol/L; +1098 µmol/L (95% CI: +431 to +1766) at 2.0 mmol/L; overall treatment effect p < 0.001. Plasma creatine overall treatment effect p = 0.002.
- Safety: 1 dropout due to a potential side effect in the 1.5 mmol/L group; no serious adverse events reported across any group; authors conclude supplementation is feasible, safe, and well-tolerated at all tested doses.

## Sleep & Recovery

### [Association Between Sleep Duration and Hypertension Development in Women: A Meta-Analysis](https://doi.org/10.29271/jcpsp.2026.08.1045)
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ · Bai Y et al. · J Coll Physicians Surg Pak (2026) · 10 articles / 12 cohort studies · meta-analysis

- Meta-analysis of 10 articles comprising 12 prospective cohort studies; databases searched: Embase, PubMed, Cochrane Library, Web of Science, CNKI, VIP, Wanfang (search up to March 15, 2024); population: women only; outcome: incident hypertension.
- Short sleep duration associated with hypertension in women: pooled RR 1.10 (95% CI: 1.06–1.13; p < 0.001); no publication bias detected for either short or long sleep groups.
- Long sleep duration was not significantly associated with hypertension risk in women: RR 1.00 (95% CI: 0.96–1.05; p = 0.958).
- Age-stratified subgroup analysis: women <50 years with short sleep — RR 1.19 (95% CI: 1.11–1.28; p < 0.001); women ≥50 years with short sleep — RR 1.06 (95% CI: 1.00–1.12; p = 0.036). Age and variability in the operational definition of "short sleep" were the principal sources of heterogeneity identified by meta-regression.
- Dose-response relationship: L-shaped curve; using 4 hours/day as reference, lowest hypertension risk at approximately 7.5 hours/day of sleep.

### [Polygraphic evaluation of the effects of functional orthodontic treatment with twin block and Herbst appliances on sleep quality: a randomized clinical trial](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11325-026-03778-w)
⭐⭐⭐⭐ · Gokce G et al. · Sleep Breath (2026) · n = 46 · RCT

- RCT; n = 46 patients with mandibular retrognathia and confirmed obstructive sleep apnea (OSA); randomly assigned to Twin Block (TB) or Herbst appliance groups; follow-up mean ~8 months; sleep assessment by polygraphy; self-reported sleep quality by Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index (PSQI).
- AHI (apnea-hypopnea index): statistically significant change from baseline to follow-up in Herbst group (mean change 1.08; 95% CI: −0.47 to 2.63; p = 0.031); TB group did not reach significance.
- Supine AHI: significant improvement in Herbst group (mean change 3.29; 95% CI: −0.20 to 6.78; p = 0.001); TB group change non-significant (mean change 0.39; 95% CI: −0.59 to 1.37; p = 0.531).
- Oxygen desaturation index (ODI): significant improvement in Herbst group (mean change 2.09; 95% CI: 0.58–3.60; p = 0.013); minimum oxygen saturation did not change significantly in either group (Herbst: p = 0.166; TB: p = 0.680).
- PSQI scores decreased significantly in both groups following treatment (p < 0.05 for both); skeletal sagittal measurements (SNB, ANB, Wits) improved significantly in both groups (p < 0.001).

### [Effect of Sleep Report Feedback With Health Guidance Using a Sleep Monitoring Device for Improving Sleep in Community-Dwelling Older People: A Three-Arm Randomized Controlled Trial](https://doi.org/10.1111/ggi.70756)
⭐⭐⭐⭐ · Chikama H et al. · Geriatr Gerontol Int (2026) · n = 229 · three-arm RCT

- Three-arm RCT conducted among community-dwelling adults in Japan; n = 229 (Group A: n = 80; Group B: n = 74; Group C: n = 75); mean age 78.5 years; 37.1% male; mean baseline Athens Insomnia Scale (AIS) score: 6.5; all participants used a wearable sleep monitoring device.
- Group A: sleep report feedback + telephone-based health guidance for 6 months. Group B: same as A for first 3 months, then sleep report feedback only for months 3–6. Group C (control): sleep report feedback alone for 6 months.
- Primary outcome (AIS score): linear mixed model analysis revealed significant group-by-time interaction (p = 0.002); both Groups A and B showed significant within-group AIS improvements at 3 months vs baseline; Group C (device-only) showed no significant change over time.
- Between-group comparison: Group B showed significantly lower AIS scores than Group C at 3 months; Group A vs C comparison not specified as reaching significance in the abstract.
- Limitations: Japanese community-dwelling older adult sample (mean age 78.5 years) limits generalizability to younger populations; telephone guidance content (sleep hygiene + daytime activity) not described in detail; no objective polysomnography data.

### [PEG-400-Modified transethosomes for resveratrol delivery: Enhanced dermal penetration and collagen remodeling over Dermarolling Strategies](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejpb.2026.115206)
⭐⭐⭐ · Zoweil M et al. · Eur J Pharm Biopharm (2026) · preclinical / ex vivo

- Preclinical/ex vivo formulation study; developed polyethylene glycol-based deformable liposomes (PEG-liposomes) as a novel topical delivery vehicle for resveratrol; size 320.17 ± 0.25 nm; zeta potential −35.2 ± 0.4 mV; a subset coated with 0.1% hyaluronic acid (HA).
- Ex vivo and confocal microscopy confirmed that PEG-liposomes reached the deep dermis; sequential application of HA-coated + uncoated vesicles: uncoated vesicles transiently disrupted lipid structures, facilitating deeper penetration of HA-coated versions.
- Combined with micro-needling (derma roller): the dual-vesicle system triggered collagen remodeling and increased superoxide dismutase (SOD) activity; simultaneously reduced oxidative stress markers MDA, MMP-1, and β-galactosidase (aging marker).
- All experiments conducted ex vivo or in animal skin models; no human clinical trial data; no quantitative effect sizes with confidence intervals or p-values for the primary collagen/antioxidant outcomes reported in the abstract.
- Claims of outperforming "commercial resveratrol products" and "existing market-leading standards" are based on preclinical ex vivo comparisons only; no head-to-head human RCT data available.
