TL;DR — Top 5 Highlights This Week

  1. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Short sleep raises hypertension risk in women — A 12-cohort meta-analysis (n not reported per study) found short sleep duration associated with hypertension risk (RR 1.10, 95% CI 1.06–1.13); the optimal sleep duration was ~7.5 hours/day (L-shaped dose-response curve).
  2. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Ketone monoester partially protects cognitive performance at altitude — In a double-blind crossover RCT (n = 16 males), R-BD R-βHB KME (650 mg/kg) attenuated hypoxia-induced mean reaction time decline by 25.5 ms (p = 0.002) vs placebo at simulated 6,096 m altitude.
  3. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Intradialytic creatine supplementation is feasible and safe in hemodialysis patients — A dose-escalation pilot RCT (n = 16) confirmed significant increases in intra-erythrocytic creatine at 1.5 mmol/L and 2.0 mmol/L (overall treatment effect p < 0.001) with one dropout across all groups.
  4. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Herbst appliance reduces AHI in mandibular retrognathia with OSA — An 8-month RCT (n = 46) found AHI significantly improved in the Herbst group (p = 0.031) and supine AHI (p = 0.001); Twin Block did not reach statistical significance for AHI.
  5. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Sleep monitoring + telephone guidance improves insomnia scores in older adults — A three-arm RCT (n = 229, mean age 78.5 years) demonstrated significant group-by-time interaction for Athens Insomnia Scale scores (p = 0.002), with improvements at 3 months vs no change in the device-only control group.
