TL;DR — Top 5 Highlights This Week

  1. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Diabetes triples all-cause mortality in alcohol-associated liver disease — A systematic review of 132,054 patients finds diabetes (aHR 3.00), obesity, and metabolic syndrome consistently worsen hepatic and extrahepatic outcomes in ALD; the case for CMRF management in this population is now robust.

  2. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Protein + resistance training beats synbiotics for muscle strength in older diabetics — A triple-blind RCT (n=40 completers) finds protein supplementation + RT improves muscle strength, insulin resistance, and oxidative stress markers; adding synbiotics provided no incremental benefit.

  3. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Vitamin D shows linear, dose-response protection against postpartum depression — Each 1 ng/mL increase in perinatal 25(OH)D reduces PPD risk by 25% (OR 0.75, 95% CI 0.68–0.82) in a cohort of 326 women; PPD incidence was 23.31%.

  4. ⭐⭐⭐ Osteoarthritis is congruently linked to depression, osteoporosis, and hypertension post-diagnosis — A multinational ComOA study (845,373 OA cases, 2,556,243 controls across UK, Netherlands, Sweden, Spain) identifies 10 pre-diagnosis and 3 post-diagnosis comorbidities with consistent cross-national associations.

  5. ⭐⭐⭐ A bacterial enzyme (LplA) extends lifespan and improves metabolic health in C. elegans and mice by boosting energy metabolism without increasing oxidative stress — early but mechanistically novel longevity research from Science Advances.
