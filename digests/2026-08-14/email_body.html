TL;DR — Top 5 Highlights

  1. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Oral GLP-1RA VCT220 (Phase II RCT) — A once-daily nonpeptide oral GLP-1 receptor agonist produced −5.75% to −9.73% body weight loss over 16 weeks vs −1.61% with placebo in 250 randomised adults. Supports phase III advancement.

  2. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Physical Activity for Adolescent Obesity (Cochrane) — 30 RCTs (n = 1,508) confirm physical activity produces modest body-weight reductions (MD −1.88 kg) in obese adolescents, but all evidence is rated low-to-very-low certainty due to high risk of bias across studies.

  3. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Probiotics/Synbiotics in Pediatric Obesity (Meta-Analysis) — 16 RCTs show probiotic/synbiotic supplementation produces only a modest BMI reduction (MD −0.91 kg/m²) with no significant effect on BMI z-score, waist circumference, body fat, or glucose metrics; GRADE certainty low.

  4. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Sleep Quality and Glycaemic Control in T2DM (Cohort) — In 1,421 adults with T2DM, each 1-point increase in PSQI score was associated with 0.096 mmol/L higher fasting glucose and 1.10× higher odds of fasting glucose ≥7.0 mmol/L, independently of confounders.

  5. ⭐⭐⭐ GLP-1RA-Induced Gastroparesis (Systematic Review) — 12 case reports (13 patients) document symptomatic gastroparesis with GLP-1RAs (semaglutide most common); all cases resolved upon drug discontinuation, highlighting a clinically underrecognised adverse event.
