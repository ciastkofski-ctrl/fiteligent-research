TL;DR — Top 5 Highlights

  1. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Creatine + resistance training in postmenopausal women — A 7-RCT meta-analysis finds creatine ≥5 g/day with RT produces small but meaningful gains in lean mass and upper-body strength; bone density evidence remains inconclusive. (Naddafha et al., J Int Soc Sports Nutr, 2026)

  2. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Migrant workers carry a dual malnutrition burden — A 30-study meta-analysis (n = 135,404) finds pooled overweight/obesity prevalence of 43.7% (95% CI: 39.8–47.6%) alongside 4.7% underweight; destination country income level is a key moderating factor. (Nurrahma et al., J Glob Health, 2026)

  3. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 2024 Endocrine Society Vitamin D Guideline — critical appraisal — RCT-derived evidence supports empiric vitamin D supplementation for children aged 1–18, adults ≥75 years, pregnant women, and those with prediabetes, but dosage thresholds and testing implications remain contested. (Pilz et al., Nutrients, 2026)

  4. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Probiotics reduce vaginal pH but not inflammatory cytokines — A 15-study meta-analysis finds probiotics significantly lower vaginal pH (OR = 0.36, 95% CI: 0.15–0.89; p = 0.03) but do not significantly alter IL-4, IL-6, IL-10, TNF-α, or Nugent score. (Xu et al., Afr J Reprod Health, 2026)

  5. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Nocebo suggestion impairs cognitive performance in older adults online — Negative verbal suggestions about an inert acoustic intervention worsened objective oddball-task performance in adults aged 65–80 without a corresponding shift in expectancy; placebo suggestions affected only subjective beliefs. (Barbon et al., Psychol Res, 2026)
