TL;DR — Top 5 Highlights This Week

  1. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ TRE + energy restriction attenuates early adaptive thermogenesis — In adults with severe obesity, adding time-restricted eating to continuous energy restriction significantly reduced early adaptive thermogenesis (−13 vs −83 kcal; p = 0.038), though RMR differences at 1 week were non-significant. Hunger suppression was a modest added benefit.

  2. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Bovine colostrum acutely modifies innate immune responses to prolonged exercise — A double-blind RCT in 16 active males found ~15% greater fMLP-stimulated neutrophil oxidative burst with colostrum vs placebo, alongside higher salivary lysozyme and neutrophil elastase release post-exercise.

  3. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ VYC-25L hyaluronic acid filler achieves 71% responder rate for jawline restoration — A meta-analysis of 2 RCTs and 3 cohort studies found significant improvement on the ALJDS scale (71%; 95% CI 48–94%; p < 0.001), with a predictable, mostly mild adverse event profile.

  4. ⭐⭐⭐ Whole-night gentle rocking reduces sleep fragmentation and improves objective sleep efficiency in individuals with subjective insomnia complaints — decreased time awake, reduced N1 sleep, and boosted NREM spindles and slow oscillations were observed in 16 poor-sleeper participants.

  5. ⭐⭐⭐ PCOS + metabolic syndrome prevalence is 57.9% in a cross-sectional cohort of 313 Algerian women — associated with higher BMI, fasting glucose, triglycerides, LH, testosterone, AMH, and lower HDL-C, progesterone, and vitamin D.
