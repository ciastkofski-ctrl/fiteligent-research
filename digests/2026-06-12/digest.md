## Obesity & Metabolic Health

### [Effect of Time-Restricted Eating on Metabolic Adaptation in Adults With Severe Obesity During Early Phase of Weight Loss](https://doi.org/10.1111/jhn.70293)

⭐⭐⭐⭐ · de Moraes Campos et al. · J Hum Nutr Diet (2026) · RCT

- 1-week RCT comparing continuous energy restriction (CER) alone vs. combined TRE + energy restriction (TRE + ER) in adults with severe obesity (BMI > 40 kg/m²); n reported as 100 participants in the abstract introduction though not confirmed in the structured fields.
- Primary outcome: resting metabolic rate (RMR) after 1 week, adjusted for baseline RMR and body composition; group difference β = 47 ± 32 kcal/day (p = 0.148) — non-significant.
- Early adaptive thermogenesis (AT) was significantly lower in TRE + ER vs. CER: −13 ± 160 kcal vs. −83 ± 150 kcal (p = 0.038).
- Leptin, insulin, and TSH all decreased from baseline in both groups after 1 week (time effect p < 0.05); no group × time interaction was observed for any hormonal marker.
- Hunger increased after 1 week only in the CER group (p = 0.048); no statistically significant hunger increase in the TRE + ER group.

## Longevity

### [Intravitreal Delivery of NGF-Chitosan Hydrogel Confers Retinal Ganglion Cell Protection and Visual Function Recovery in Experimental Glaucoma](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bioactmat.2026.04.037)

⭐⭐⭐ · Gao et al. · Bioact Mater (2026)

- Animal study (rat model of ocular hypertension-induced glaucoma); NGF loaded into a chitosan hydrogel was injected intravitreally.
- NGF-chitosan hydrogel significantly enhanced RGC survival following glaucomatous injury by approximately 27% compared with lesion control group.
- Mechanistically, treatment upregulated NGF and its high-affinity receptor TrkA in the retina; suppressed retinal glial activation; and enhanced mTOR signaling.
- Preserved dendritic architecture and long-distance axonal projections, and promoted recovery of visual function as assessed by visual electrophysiology and behavioral measures.
- Nestin activation was also observed, suggesting potential neural progenitor involvement; preclinical only — no human data.

### [Activation-Induced Cytidine Deaminase Promotes DNA Demethylation and Expression of the Ninjurin-2 Gene](https://doi.org/10.1242/bio.062001)

⭐⭐⭐ · Okada et al. · Biol Open (2026)

- In vitro and in vivo mouse study; AID overexpression in colorectal cancer cell line DLD-1 induced expression of 619 genes, including pathways involving PI3K-AKT, mTOR, and MAPK signaling.
- NINJ2 gene expression increased more than fivefold in AID-overexpressing DLD-1 cells; conversely, NINJ2 was reduced in colitis-induced colonic epithelial cells (CECs) of AID-knockout mice vs. wild-type.
- AID binds to the NINJ2 promoter/enhancer region and induces DNA demethylation, associated with transcriptional upregulation.
- Recovery from induced colitis was significantly delayed in AID-knockout mice, suggesting AID-NINJ2 signaling may facilitate wound healing in intestinal inflammation.
- Relevance to human longevity biology is indirect; mechanistic findings in mouse colitis model.

## Strength & Hypertrophy

### [Ambulatory Hypertension and Left Ventricular Hypertrophy in Pediatric Chronic Kidney Disease: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00431-026-07153-5)

⭐⭐⭐ · Chung et al. · Eur J Pediatr (2026) · n = 6,509 · systematic review and meta-analysis

- 51 full-text studies (n = 6,509 children with CKD) eligible for inclusion; literature search covered 1974–2025 across five databases; PROSPERO registration CRD42023426891.
- Pediatric CKD patients with ambulatory hypertension had increased left ventricular mass index (LVMI) with a mean difference of 5.15 g/m² (exact CIs not reported in available abstract text) compared with normotensive CKD patients.
- Odds of LVH were significantly higher in the hypertensive group; children with 24-h MAP and SBP above the 90th percentile showed significantly greater LVMI.
- Significant heterogeneity noted among studies with respect to study design and outcome definitions; results should be interpreted with caution given this variability.
- Clinical implication: supports integration of ambulatory blood pressure monitoring (ABPM) into routine pediatric CKD care for cardiovascular risk stratification; does not directly address exercise or strength training.

### [Association Between Amyloid Deposition and Collagen-to-Elastin Ratio in the Ligamentum Flavum: A Machine Learning Histologic Study in Matched Groups](https://doi.org/10.1227/neu.0000000000004123)

⭐⭐⭐ · Patel et al. · Neurosurgery (2026) · n = 64

- Case-control study; 64 patients with lumbar stenosis (32 amyloid-positive, 32 age- and diagnosis-matched amyloid-negative controls) drawn from a database of 324 consecutive cases; amyloid status confirmed by Congo Red staining and mass spectrometry.
- Amyloid-positive LF demonstrated a greater collagen-to-elastin ratio of 1.36 vs. 1.05 in amyloid-negative patients (p = 0.014).
- The higher ratio was primarily driven by lower elastin content: elastin occupied 7.5% less tissue area in amyloid-positive LF (p = 0.008).
- Average LF thickness on T2-weighted MRI was 0.538 mm greater in amyloid-positive patients.
- Random forest pixel classifier in QuPath used for collagen/elastin quantification; Bland-Altman analysis showed <1% bias vs. manual segmentation supervised by a board-certified pathologist.

## Supplements

### [Effects of Acute Bovine Colostrum Supplementation on Immune Responses to Prolonged Cycling: A Randomised Crossover Trial](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00394-026-04016-5)

⭐⭐⭐⭐ · Jones et al. · Eur J Nutr (2026) · n = 16 · RCT (double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover)

- Double-blind, placebo-controlled, randomized crossover trial in 16 healthy, recreationally active males; exercise protocol: 2.5 hours of cycling at 15% Δ (delta) intensity.
- Dosing protocol: bovine colostrum consumed 1 h pre-exercise (30 g), immediately pre-exercise (5 g), and midway through exercise (5 g); compared to an isoenergetic placebo.
- fMLP-stimulated blood neutrophil oxidative burst was approximately 15% greater across timepoints with bovine colostrum vs. placebo (trial effect, p < 0.05).
- Salivary lysozyme concentration and bacterial-stimulated neutrophil elastase release were both higher in the colostrum trial at post-exercise and 1 h post-exercise respectively (trial × time interaction, p < 0.05).
- No significant effect on leukocyte trafficking, PMA-stimulated neutrophil oxidative burst, salivary secretory IgA, or salivary lactoferrin; mechanistic sub-study confirmed that plasma obtained post-colostrum consumption directly enhanced neutrophil oxidative burst ex vivo.

### [Prevalence and Clinical Correlates of Metabolic Syndrome Among Women With Polycystic Ovary Syndrome in Western Algeria: A Cross-Sectional Study](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2025-115090)

⭐⭐⭐ · Messaoud et al. · BMJ Open (2026) · n = 313

- Cross-sectional study; 313 women aged 16–45 years diagnosed with PCOS per Rotterdam 2004 criteria; recruited from outpatient endocrinology and gynaecology services in the Oran region of western Algeria.
- 181 of 313 participants (57.9%) met criteria for metabolic syndrome.
- Women with metabolic syndrome had significantly higher BMI (26.70 ± 5.93 vs. 25.06 ± 6.47 kg/m²; p = 0.004), elevated fasting glucose (133.43 ± 28.52 vs. 105.41 ± 28.54 mg/dL; p < 0.0001), higher triglycerides (p < 0.0001), and lower HDL-C (p < 0.0001).
- Hormonal differences in the metabolic syndrome group: elevated LH, LH/FSH ratio, total testosterone, and AMH; reduced progesterone and vitamin D (all p < 0.0001).
- Clinical features significantly more prevalent in the metabolic syndrome group: hyperandrogenism, menstrual irregularity, and secondary infertility; single-region sample limits generalizability.

## Sleep & Recovery

### [Prisoners of the Body: The Link Between Interoception and Sleep Paralysis](https://doi.org/10.1111/jsr.70393)

⭐⭐⭐ · Forte et al. · J Sleep Res (2026) · n = 210

- Cross-sectional observational study in 210 Italian adults; examined prevalence, phenomenology, and cognitive-emotional characteristics of sleep paralysis (SP), plus measures of interoception.
- 1 in 4 participants (25%) reported at least one episode of SP; common sensations included inability to move or speak, tingling, sealed eyelids, vivid cognitive scenes, felt presence, and fear of death.
- SP was not significantly associated with poorer sleep quality or chronic anxiety — contrary to earlier findings.
- SP was associated with insomnia symptoms and higher interoceptive sensitivity (hyper-attuned body awareness); individuals with SP showed greater attention to and emotion regulation through subtle bodily cues.
- Study is observational and cross-sectional; directionality of the interoception–SP association cannot be established; Italian population sample.

### [Whole-Night Gentle Rocking Improves Sleep in Poor Sleepers With Insomnia Complaints](https://doi.org/10.1111/jsr.70392)

⭐⭐⭐ · Perrault et al. · J Sleep Res (2026) · n = 16

- Within-subject crossover design; 16 young adults with subjective difficulties initiating and/or maintaining sleep and objectively poor sleep quality; each participant completed a habituation night plus one rocking and one stationary sleep night in the laboratory.
- Continuous rocking stimulation applied at 0.25 Hz (lateral movement) throughout the entire night; outcome measures included PSG-based sleep architecture, declarative memory consolidation, and subjective sleep quality.
- Rocking decreased sleep fragmentation, time spent awake, and time in N1 (light) sleep; objective sleep efficiency and subjective sleep quality both increased vs. stationary condition.
- Neural entrainment effect replicated from prior good-sleeper studies: rocking boosted NREM fast spindles and slow oscillations.
- Changes in sleep architecture did not translate to improved overnight declarative memory performance; n = 16 limits statistical power; laboratory conditions may not reflect home sleep environment.

### [Sodium Oxybate Alters Sleep Architecture and Reduces Emotional Selectivity in Memory-Related Responses](https://doi.org/10.1111/jsr.70386)

⭐⭐⭐ · Colin et al. · J Sleep Res (2026) · n = 19

- Double-blind, randomized, crossover study in 19 healthy young men; two monitored laboratory nights (sodium oxybate [SXB] vs. placebo); fMRI used the following afternoon during a recognition memory task.
- SXB increased NREM sleep percentage and delta power; reduced REM sleep percentage and theta power; did not affect overall sleep efficiency.
- Overall recognition memory performance did not differ between SXB and placebo conditions.
- Autonomic/emotional response finding: pupil diameter increased selectively for neutral pictures after SXB night (not for emotional pictures); this reduced emotional selectivity was associated with the degree of REM sleep reduction (smaller REM reduction → larger differential pupillary response).
- fMRI: amygdala, hippocampus, locus coeruleus, and orbitofrontal cortex showed stronger responses to emotional vs. neutral stimuli after placebo but not after SXB; orbitofrontal responses correlated with REM sleep in placebo condition only. All-male sample; results may not generalize.

## Skin & Hair Health

### [Safe and Effective Restoration of Jawline Definition With Hyaluronic Acid Injectable Gel: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42241633/)

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ · Hashemloo & Milanifard · Acta Dermatovenerol Alp Pannonica Adriat (2026) · meta-analysis

- Systematic review and meta-analysis; included 2 randomized controlled trials and 3 cohort studies sourced from PubMed, EMBASE, Web of Science, and Cochrane Library (January 2015 – May 2025); product evaluated: VYC-25L (Allergan), a hyaluronic acid-based injectable soft tissue filler.
- VYC-25L group had significantly higher responder rates on the Allergan Loss of Jawline Definition Scale (ALJDS) compared to control: 71% (p < 0.001; 95% CI 48–94%).
- Most common adverse events: lumps/bumps (ES: 83%; 95% CI 66–99%), swelling (ES: 81%; 95% CI 69–92%), pain after injection (ES: 79%; 95% CI 62–96%), and tenderness to touch (ES: 76%; 95% CI 61–92%).
- All adverse events were described as predictable and consistent with soft tissue filler profile; no serious adverse events were reported across included studies.
- Limitation: only 5 studies included; heterogeneity of cohort study designs; industry-related studies may carry sponsorship bias risk; no DOI available in the source record.

### [Safety and Effectiveness of Two Reconstitution Volumes of Poly-L-Lactic Acid for Correction of Décolletage Wrinkles](https://doi.org/10.36849/JDD.9928)

⭐⭐⭐⭐ · Joseph et al. · J Drugs Dermatol (2026) · n = 30 · RCT (open-label)

- 9-month open-label RCT; 30 women with moderate/severe wrinkles on the Galderma Décolletage Scale (GDS) randomized 1:1 to PLLA-SCA (Sculptra®) reconstituted with either 8 mL or 17 mL sterile water + 1 mL 2% lidocaine; up to 4 treatments, 1 month apart.
- Primary endpoint at month 9: majority of subjects in both groups showed ≥1-point improvement on investigator-assessed GDS (8 mL arm: 93.8%; 17 mL arm: 78.6%); ≥93% improved at month 6.
- Global Aesthetic Improvement Scale (GAIS) improvement exceeded 90% of subjects in both groups at months 6 and 9.
- Subject satisfaction with wrinkle appearance exceeded 90% by month 3 in both groups, sustained through month 9; >80% satisfied with texture, smoothness, and radiance at months 6 and 9.
- One adverse event judged treatment-related; no serious AEs; no discontinuations due to AEs; small n = 30 and open-label design limit conclusions.

### [Effects of a Mixed Herbal and Fruit Dietary Supplement Shot on Antioxidant Status, Oxidative Stress Parameters, Skin Parameters, and Safety Profiles in Middle-Aged Women: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tjnut.2026.101574)

⭐⭐⭐⭐ · Settakorn et al. · J Nutr (2026) · n = 30 · RCT (double-blind, placebo-controlled)

- 12-week double-blind, placebo-controlled RCT; 30 middle-aged women randomized to a mixed herbal and fruit dietary supplement shot (HERB) or placebo (PLA); Thai Clinical Trials Registry TCTR20241103003.
- Primary outcomes: antioxidant status and oxidative stress parameters; skin parameters and safety profiles assessed as secondary outcomes; analyzed by intention-to-treat and per-protocol approaches.
- At week 12, HERB significantly increased superoxide dismutase (SOD) activity vs. PLA (mean difference = 7.99 U/L; 95% CI: 1.29–14.70; p = 0.0212).
- HERB significantly reduced facial porphyrin intensity vs. PLA (mean difference = −44.56; 95% CI: −76.06 to −13.60; p = 0.0073); all other antioxidant, oxidative stress, and skin parameters showed no significant between-group differences.
- Gastrointestinal disturbance more frequent in PLA group (8 vs. 1 participant; p = 0.0142); all AEs mild and self-resolving; n = 30 and 12-week duration limit generalizability; authors caution against over-interpretation.
