TL;DR — Top 5 Highlights This Week

  1. ⭐⭐⭐ Semaglutide in Type 1 Diabetes (real-world): In 879 Danish T1D patients using semaglutide off-label, HbA1c fell 5.7 mmol/mol (0.52%) at 6 months with no increase in DKA or hypoglycemia hospitalizations vs. matched controls — but only 50% of users were still adherent at one year.
  2. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Cancer cachexia meta-analysis (78 studies, n=10,502): Systemic cancer treatment — especially chemotherapy — is consistently associated with skeletal muscle mass decline (SMC = −0.24, 95% CI −0.29 to −0.20), approximately −5% over ~90 days. Massive heterogeneity (I²=92%) limits a single pooled interpretation.
  3. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ CBT-I for perinatal depression prevention (RCT protocol): A fully remote, 443-participant RCT is underway testing digital CBT-I (SleepioRx) vs. sleep hygiene education for preventing postpartum depression through 12 months; primary endpoint is structured DSM-5 depression diagnosis.
  4. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ CBT in fibromyalgia (RCT, n=70): 10-week individualized CBT added to routine treatment significantly improved anxiety (HADS-A: 13→10, p<0.001), fibromyalgia impact (FIQ: 71.80→51.52, p<0.001), and hopelessness (BHS: 7→4, p<0.001) vs. routine treatment alone.
  5. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Novel recombinant BoNT/A vs. Botox (Phase III RCT, n=529): YY001 achieved an 86.7% composite response rate for glabellar lines at Week 4 vs. 66.7% for Botox® (p<0.0001), with faster onset and median duration of 97 days.
