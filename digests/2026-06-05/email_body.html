TL;DR — Top 5 Highlights

  1. ⭐⭐⭐ Tirzepatide after bariatric surgery failure — 34 post-bariatric patients achieved a mean 18.1% total body weight loss at 24 weeks with tirzepatide; all adverse events were mild GI and none led to discontinuation. Practical signal for a common clinical dead-end.

  2. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Probiotics/synbiotics/berberine in T2DM — Largest meta-analysis to date (>30 RCTs, >2,000 participants) shows statistically significant but clinically modest glycemic benefits: FPG −0.71 mmol/L, HbA1c −0.19%. Adjunctive role only.

  3. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Fisetin + concurrent training in obese men — 12-week RCT (n=44) combining 200 mg/day fisetin with interval resistance-aerobic training raised Maresin-1 and reduced IL-6, TNF-α, fasting glucose, insulin, and HOMA-IR beyond training alone.

  4. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Sleep deprivation shifts fat oxidation during exercise — Crossover RCT (n=32): total and partial sleep deprivation increased peak fat oxidation rates during an incremental cycling test the following morning in recreationally trained adults, while carbohydrate oxidation fell.

  5. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Personalized app ± coaching RCT — 3-month trial (n=209 office workers/bus drivers) showed both app groups added ~14–16 min/week exercise; only the app+coaching group also improved vegetable intake (+51.8 g/day) and sleep duration (+0.40 h/night).
