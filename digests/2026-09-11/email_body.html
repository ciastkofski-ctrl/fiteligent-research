TL;DR — Top 5 Highlights

  1. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Iodine supplementation in children (Cochrane): High-certainty evidence that iodine supplementation produces a slight but real cognitive benefit (SMD 0.19); moderate-certainty evidence for large reductions in hypothyroidism (RR 0.03) and infant all-cause mortality (RR 0.32).

  2. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Hydrolyzed collagen for skin (SLR of 25 RCTs): Improvements in hydration (10/15 trials), elasticity (10/13 trials), and wrinkle depth (9/10 trials) over placebo — but most trials carry high risk of bias; evidence is preliminary.

  3. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ DLMEH (D-leucine prodrug) for sarcopenia: Preclinical compound restores dexamethasone-suppressed muscle protein synthesis by 58.2% in human myotubes at 100 μM — 8× lower dose than L-leucine's 28.5% rescue; first-in-class Sestrin2-targeting mTORC1 activator, no human data yet.

  4. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Rapamycin in APOE4 carriers: Low-dose rapamycin (1 mg/day, 4 weeks) increased cerebral blood flow >15% across multiple brain regions in APOE4 carriers (n=9); non-carriers showed no significant CBF change — genotype-dependent effect in a small pilot.

  5. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Fractional laser vs. microneedling for scars: Across 9 RCTs, fractional laser shows statistically significant advantage over microneedling in scar scores (SMD = −0.99) and collagen fiber regeneration (SMD = −2.14), though substantial heterogeneity limits conclusions.
