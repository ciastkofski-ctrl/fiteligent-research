TL;DR — Top 5 Highlights

  1. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ GLP-1 RAs & pregnancy hypertension (n = 10,880): Periconceptional or first-trimester GLP-1 RA exposure is not significantly associated with hypertensive disorders of pregnancy (OR 0.91, 95% CI 0.57–1.47). Reassuring signal, but very limited data from only 3 retrospective cohorts.

  2. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Omega-3 & diabetic kidney disease: Mendelian randomization across 12 RCTs confirms a causal protective association between omega-3 levels and DKD risk (OR 0.869, 95% CI 0.772–0.978), with supplementation reducing triglycerides (−0.27 mmol/L), systolic BP (−4.50 mmHg), and kidney injury molecule-1 (−1.74 pg/mL).

  3. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Exercise improves sleep in psychiatric patients (n = 1,628): Meta-analysis of 21 RCTs shows a moderate overall effect (Hedges' g = −0.57), with aerobic exercise at low-to-moderate intensity ≥3×/week driving the largest gains.

  4. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Core stability vs. foot-ankle training in knee OA (n = 100): 8-week core stability program produced clinically meaningful superiority over foot-ankle strengthening on pain (VAS) and all KOOS subscales, with large effect sizes favoring proximal training.

  5. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ FRF + tretinoin for striae albae: Combination fractional radiofrequency + topical tretinoin was the only treatment achieving a statistically significant patient-reported improvement over untreated control (POSAS-PT p = 0.029), though objective blinded dermatologist assessments did not reach significance.
